About Us

ENTYTEC

We aim to bring joy to people's lives by creating experiences worth sharing and supporting other creators along the way.

Entytec is a game studio based in the Dominican Republic dedicated to crafting fun, meaningful, and visually striking interactive experiences. From small prototypes to full releases, we focus on gameplay that inspires curiosity and sparks connection between players and creators.

We also collaborate with new talents through internships and partnerships, helping aspiring developers grow in the game industry.
